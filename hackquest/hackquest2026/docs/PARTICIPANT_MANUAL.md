# Participant Manual — HACKQUEST CTF 2026

## Before the event

1. Register at `/register` with **2–4 participants**. Every email must be unique across the whole
   event — a participant cannot join two teams.
2. Wait for organiser approval, then log in at `/login` with the team name and password.
3. Your dashboard shows the team name, members, team ID and your unique access code `HQ-XXXXXX`.
   All members see the same code. It proves team identity; it does **not** replace the password.

## During the event

- The current round and its timer are driven by server time. Refreshing or reconnecting never
  resets them.
- Future rounds remain locked until an organiser opens them for every team at once.
- Open a challenge to read its description, download any attachment and reveal hints.
- Submit flags in the format `HQCTF{...}`. Correct submissions award points immediately;
  incorrect submissions give no hint about the answer and are rate-limited.
- Solved challenges are marked ✓. Your score and progress update on the next poll.
- Riddle and meme breaks are available from the navigation; they do not affect official scoring.

## Safety

All challenges use synthetic files, fictional logs and toy applications. Do not attack real
websites, public infrastructure, college systems or other teams. The platform is the only target.
