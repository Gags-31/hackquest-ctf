Event Overview

HackQuest CTF is a hands-on cybersecurity competition introducing practical web security concepts. Teams solve intentionally vulnerable challenges, find hidden flags (e.g. CTF{hackquest_web_warrior}), and submit them on the platform to earn points.

Duration: 4h 30m · Team Size: 2–4 · 15 challenges · Max 3,000 points

Objectives & Format

  • Introduce practical cybersecurity & web vulnerabilities
  • Develop analytical problem-solving skills & ethical hacking practices
  • Promote teamwork and technical collaboration

Jeopardy-style: each challenge has a description, difficulty, points, target, optional hints, and a flag field.

Rules & Ethical Guidelines

  • Only provided HackQuest systems may be targeted
  • No attacking the CTF platform, other teams, or DoS/DDoS
  • No targeting college systems or external websites
  • Flags must not be shared; don't interfere with other teams' environments
  • Report infrastructure vulnerabilities to organizers
  • Violations may result in disqualification
