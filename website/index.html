root@hackquest:~$ Event Overview

HackQuest CTF is a hands-on cybersecurity competition that throws you into a gauntlet of intentionally vulnerable systems. Investigate targets, exploit weaknesses, capture hidden flags, and submit them to score points. From basic web recon to blind SQLi, SSRF and JWT forgery — every challenge is a real technique used in the wild.

Whether you're breaking CTFs for the first time or hunting flags for years, the difficulty curve is built for you. Teams of 2–4. 3h 45m on the clock. Highest score takes the crown.
