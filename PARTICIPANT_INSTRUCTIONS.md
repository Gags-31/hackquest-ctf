# HackQuest CTF — Participant Instructions

## Before the Event
- Bring a laptop with a modern browser (Chrome/Firefox), and dev tools extensions you like (e.g., Burp Suite Community, a cookie editor).
- Teams: 2–4 participants. Register as a team.

## On Event Day
1. Arrive before 10:00 AM for registration & team verification.
2. Receive your Team ID and CTF platform credentials.
3. Attend the 10:20 welcome and 10:35 rules/platform demo.
4. Complete the practice challenge ("Inspect Me") — view page source, find the demo flag, submit it.
5. Competition runs 11:00 AM – 2:45 PM.

## How to Play
1. Log in to the CTF platform.
2. Pick a challenge — note its description, difficulty, and points.
3. Investigate the target (web app / file / environment).
4. Find the vulnerability, capture the flag (format: `CTF{...}`).
5. Submit on the platform: correct = points awarded; wrong = try again.
6. Hints are available — each costs a 25-point penalty.
7. Track your ranking on the live scoreboard.

## Scoring
Easy 100 · Medium 200 · Advanced 300 · Hard 400. Max 3,000 points.
Tie-breaker: whoever reaches the score first ranks higher.

## Tips
- Start with Easy challenges to warm up.
- Split roles: one on recon/dev tools, one on payload ideas, one on notes.
- Keep a shared doc of attempted payloads and found flags.
- Read error messages — they often leak information.

## Rules (Summary)
- Attack only the provided HackQuest targets. No DoS/DDoS.
- Don't attack the CTF platform, other teams, college systems, or external sites.
- Never share flags between teams.
- Report infrastructure issues to organizers immediately.
- Violations → disqualification. Organizers' decision is final.

Good luck, and hack ethically! 🔐
